# @pepbits/healthcare

## 0.2.0 (prepared, unpublished)

- New package, added 27 October 2026 directly into the prepared fixed 0.2.0 group (no separate version bump): controlled clinical form sections (patient banner, vital signs with live NEWS2 preview, allergy review and add allergy, arrival and coded complaint, screening), the profile-driven `IntakeForm`, and `EncounterQueue`/`IntakeQueue`, all over a host `ClinicalDataPort`. The first profile is outpatient nurse intake, rebuilt from the AllyVora clinical workspace with unchanged validation and payloads.
- Emergency triage (27 October 2026, same unpublished 0.2.0): `EMERGENCY_TRIAGE` profile, triage model (`EsiDecisions`, `withEsiAnswer`, `esiInputs`, `finalEsiLevel`, `triageProblems`, `triagePayload`), `ClinicalDataPort.previewEsi`, ESI components (`EsiSection`, `EsiDecisionPoints`, `EsiDecisionRow`, `YesNoChoice`, `EsiResourcesChoice`, `EsiResultSummary`, `EsiOverrideFields`, `AreaSelect`, `EsiLevelChip`, `useEsiPreview`) and `TriageQueue` with `TriageWaitingRow` and `EsiLevelCounts`. Existing sections are split into smaller exported parts (`ArrivalFields` and its three choices, `ComplaintField`, `CodedChip`, `PatientWordsField`, `ScreeningQuestionRow`, `ScreeningActionNotice`, `AllergyList`, `AllergyReviewChoice`, `SaveChecklist`, `ChecklistLine`, `FormSaveBar`, `QueueHeader`, `WaitTime`, `PatientFlags`, `StaleListNotice`, `useEncounterRows`). `IntakeProfile.savedMessage` now also receives the save result, and profiles can set `askArrivalMode`.
