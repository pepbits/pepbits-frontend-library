## Changed behavior and source boundary

All eight report destinations (Sales register, Purchase register, Stock summary and Receivables aging in both ERP1 and ERP2) gain the source typed advanced filters, three-state column sorting and group subtotals. Multiple choices/reference values, open date/numeric ranges and person text retain their wire types. Apply validates ranges; Cancel retains accepted conditions; Reset clears the draft. Main date presets and advanced date conditions replace each other. Groups use the complete filtered result, retain sorted row order within each group and span screen pages without changing subtotal values. CSV/XLSX exports include all filtered rows and a grand total; display-only group/subtotal headers are omitted. Both ledgers and all six matrices retain their already implemented source controls, which are audited per destination in the feature guide. Source templates have no pivot designer or comparison-period option, and matrices have no export control.

Both Invoice print destinations now measure rendered rows and partition A4 documents. Company/customer/invoice identity and item headers repeat. Carry totals include tax, item numbers are continuous, page numbers identify the total page count, and payment/tax/grand totals/English amount words/optional terms appear once on the final page. A summary-only final page is permitted. Effective font/format changes remeasure; zoom affects screen scale. Printing is disabled before measurement and when an individual row or final block cannot fit one page. Single oversized-block splitting and exact visual matching are outside this delivery.
