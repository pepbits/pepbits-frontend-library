## Data, errors and permissions

The configured transport validates relative paths and preserves Settle's structured error envelope. Business data is loaded over HTTP; frozen fictional fixtures are confined to tests. Successful writes refresh mounted queries. Aborted or obsolete reads cannot replace a different path/identity's data. Recoverable failures preserve entered values; a desk identity change clears them. No durable business drafts or new idempotent-write protocol is added; an uncertain write outcome requires checking the ledger before repeating the operation.

The source `x-user-id` switch is a fictional desk identity, separate from host sign-in, with tab-scoped selection per host identity. Settle checks rights on its server; UI disabled/hidden states do not grant authority. This is not production authentication or tenant isolation. Counter selection, pending-result/approval/lifecycle gates and simulated card-terminal approval retain the source API contract. No real payment or external provider is contacted.
