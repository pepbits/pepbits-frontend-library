## Changes and user impact

Private `@pepbits-demo/school` ports **26 Scholaris route patterns** (24 static, two dynamic) and six role dashboards. Administrator covers records/registration, admissions, classes, subjects, timetable, attendance, assignments, quizzes, exams, marks, live classes/room, whiteboard, library, fees, calendar, notices, messages, reports and settings. Teacher, Student, Parent, Librarian and Accountant receive their granted portals. Sign-in and `/preferences` use the public shared host. All destinations are listed in the source map.

Session-issued `referenceSchoolViews` plus the account's recognized school role determine available portals. A query hint selects only among those grants; even the administrator role alone does not imply six grants. The backend separately validates role headers and resource/operation access. Direct links, reloads and header switching retain accepted scope.

Domain commands preserve the source workflow: multi-step registrations, acknowledged admission moves, quiz authoring/preview/student grading, attachments/retry, marks/attendance, canonical library stock/fines and invoice receipts. Source media previews remain local; payments and automatic messages are synthetic. School metadata and definitions live inside the demo. No Scholaris/erp-config MasterTransferPanel or PricingExpressionInput usage exists to port; generic implementations are already public patterns and separate School-product integrations remain outside this source/API.
