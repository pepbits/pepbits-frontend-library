## Scope and structure

The private Next.js app has 37 static destinations: 23 Suite core/masters, 13 clinical/patient/care pages, and Preferences. Patient, encounter and master record/new links and patient-specific clinical links use the same typed renderer registry. SessionProvider authenticates against the demo API; PreferenceHost loads policy and serializes revision-aware updates; LocalizationProvider fetches the canonical shared catalog at runtime; ShellProvider supplies the full frame with healthcare navigation and API-authenticated identity. The API base is configuration. An optional server proxy forwards the contract headers. AI is off.

| Ported area | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Suite core | Dashboard, patients/registration/record, encounters/create/record, appointment board, approvals/eRx, hospital/pharmacy billing, invoices and contract pricing |
| Suite masters | Fourteen configured master destinations; public WorklistPage/RecordPage/TemplateFields replace the original generic master renderer |
| Clinical templates | Patient query, record and 360, including ClinicalPatientWorkspace; public record layout reused |
| Clinical documents | Triage, clinical consultation, comprehensive consultation and OP consultation, with product definitions and shared clinical document orchestration |
| Other clinical workflows | OP registration, clinic billing, emergency registration, inpatient admission and the two care consultation-entry designs |
| Adapters/tests | Corresponding healthcare HTTP adapters and product types; original component/API tests adapted to public providers and mocked clients; route and host regressions added |

ClinicalPatientWorkspace is used by the Healthcare product and is ported here as a demo module. Moving its product copy to the Healthcare repository is a **separate step**, not completed by this change. Existing generic record-layout tests remain in page-engines. Library-page launchers are replaced by the new routing/navigation. The four-layer `consultation` composer, ambient recorder and their specialized-editor cases are explicitly deferred together; no placeholder destination is exposed.
